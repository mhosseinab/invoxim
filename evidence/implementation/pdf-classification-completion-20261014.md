## Implemented

Supported text PDFs now pass through the existing local unpdf 1.8.1 port in direct-model mode; original-byte hashes, part IDs and page text remain distinct from model financial interpretation. Tested ordinary Flate compression, embedded fonts, direct/integer-indirect stream lengths, German characters, decimal commas, dates and independent lines. No new dependencies, providers, storage, database migration, OCR, native vendor PDF capability or secret/config generation. No PDF preview was added.

Support is deliberately a restricted conventional PDF subset. Source limits are 4 MiB / 64 parts / depth 8, 20 aggregate pages / 24,000 aggregate characters per message. Local converter validates canonical classicxref/object/generation/offset/Length/Root/Pages/Count relationships before PDF.js, rejecting ambiguous or unsupported layouts, active/external features, encryption, unsupported filters/object streams/incrementalxref and image-only scans. Inflation is bounded 8 MiB / 128 streams / 4096 objects, 100x with 64 KiB per-stream floor, 1 KiB compressed input chunks and 100k names. Ten-second cancellation/deadlines are cooperative at async boundaries; no hard synchronous CPU preemption or deployed performance guarantee is claimed. Passive links do not fetch resources.

Each readable document now receives its own mandatory Clef decision/category and document/hash/config-bound cache. A positive PDF cannot qualify an unrelated covering email; a negative body cannot discard an invoice attachment. Negative documents avoid financial extraction, while unreadable siblings/open reviews and source incompleteness remain visible. Source completion waits for pending documents. Tenant/session/consent/generation/credentials/settings/version/lease fences, old cash facts and 40 SQL invocation bounds remain. Parser/classifier policy/notice changes require refreshed processing review on rollout. Historical reviews/counters/queues are not reset, and old paid results are not silently reinterpreted or billed again. Staged incompatible old PDFs become source-change review rather than an automatic replay loop.
